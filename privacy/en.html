1. Publisher and contact

Krzysztof Fukas, a private individual, is the publisher of Rdzeń Chaosu and the controller of personal data processed in connection with providing the game and handling support requests. Contact: kfukas@gmail.com. Android application ID: pl.rdzenchaosu.game.

This policy covers the game, contact with the publisher and the game's information website. It distinguishes information stored on your device from data processed by service providers.

2. Progress and settings on your device

The game saves progress, unlocked heroes and equipment, core fragments, objectives, research, expedition records, language, audio and graphics settings locally. Your selected age range and records of granted ad rewards are also saved locally to prevent duplicate rewards.

The game has no player accounts, publisher-operated save server or cloud synchronisation. These saves are not automatically sent to the publisher. Ordinary gameplay does not ask for your name, email address or date of birth. The game has no publisher-operated analytics module or automatic crash reporting to the publisher.

3. Age and protection of minors

The game asks you to choose an age range: under 13, 13–17, 18 or older, or no answer. It does not require your full date of birth. This choice determines ad availability and stays in the game's local settings.

Ads are disabled for everyone under 18 and for unknown ages; the game does not initialise the Google Mobile Ads advertising SDK for these players. The game remains available offline, and an unavailable ad does not block gameplay. A parent or guardian may contact the publisher about a child’s data.

4. Advertising for adults

From version 0.7.11, the game may automatically show an adult player a standard full-screen ad after each completed wave, before the shop opens. No watch-ad button is required, and no bonus fragments are awarded. Ads preload in the menu or shop after age and required privacy checks. Loading may finish during combat, but an ad is only shown at a completed-wave transition. If no ad is ready or an error occurs, the game continues to the shop; a late ad does not interrupt shopping or combat. Older versions may offer optional rewarded ads. The game uses Google AdMob and Google User Messaging Platform. Test releases use demo ads.

When ads are used, Google may process your IP address and approximate location, device or application identifiers, ad interactions and diagnostic data. These support ad delivery and measurement, security and fraud prevention. SDK data is transmitted using TLS encryption.

The game's configuration disables ad personalisation and access to the Android advertising ID. This does not eliminate data processing: Google may use other identifiers and technical data. The game does not send your name, email address, a publisher-assigned player ID or locally saved progress to the advertising service.

Before initialising the advertising module, the game updates consent information and displays Google’s form where required. You can change your choices using the ad privacy button in settings when it is available for your region. No internet connection or available ad is required to continue playing. Details about Google’s processing: https://policies.google.com/privacy?hl=en and https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites?hl=en.

5. Email support and website

If you email kfukas@gmail.com, the publisher receives your email address, message and any attachments. They are used to reply, resolve your request and, if necessary, establish or defend legal claims. Include only information needed to explain the issue. Do not send passwords or payment details.

The information website is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may process technical connection data, including IP addresses and browser information; see https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement. Gmail handles email correspondence. The publisher does not add analytics, forms or ads to the website. Visiting the website does not start the game’s advertising module.

6. Purposes, legal bases and recipients

Local saves allow game features to work and retain your settings; they are not sent to the publisher. Where the publisher processes personal data, support and abuse prevention rely on the legitimate interest in providing assistance and security (GDPR Article 6(1)(f)). Processing required by law relies on Article 6(1)(c). Where additional advertising processing or access to information on a device requires consent, it is obtained before that activity; processing covered by consent relies on Article 6(1)(a).

Recipients may include Google and providers supporting hosting, email, ads and security, as needed to provide those services, and competent authorities where required by law. The publisher does not sell game saves or support correspondence data. Google's services may involve processing outside the European Economic Area; transfer safeguards are described at https://policies.google.com/privacy/frameworks?hl=en.

7. Retention and deletion

Local saves remain on your device until you clear the app's data in Android settings or uninstall the game. Clearing data also removes progress and your age-range choice. There is no player account requiring a separate deletion request.

Support correspondence is retained until the matter is resolved and afterwards only as needed to establish or defend claims or comply with a legal obligation. You can request deletion of correspondence data using the contact email. Google's retention periods depend on the policies and settings of the relevant Google services. Uninstalling the game does not automatically delete data previously processed by Google.

8. Your rights and policy changes

Subject to applicable law, you can request access, correction, deletion, restriction or portability and object to processing. You can withdraw consent without affecting earlier lawful processing. You may complain to the Polish supervisory authority, the President of UODO: https://uodo.gov.pl.

For data available to the publisher, contact kfukas@gmail.com. For data processed by Google, you can also use the tools and contacts described in Google's policy. The publisher does not make automated decisions about players that have legal or similarly significant effects.

This policy will be updated when game features or data practices change. The current version's date appears at the top of this page.
